## Problem 1

Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent random variables distributed as exponential with parameters $\lambda$ and $\mu$, respectively.  
Let $I$, independent of $X$ and $Y$, be a Bernoulli random variable with success probability $p=\frac{\mu}{\lambda+\mu}$.  
Define $W = X-Y$ and $Z=\begin{cases}
X,&\text{if }I=1\\
-Y,&\text{if }I=0\end{cases}$  

**(a) Show, by using moment generating functions, that $W$ and $Z$ have the same distribution.**  
(5 marks) 

**Solution:**  

- 计算 $X$ 的矩母函数:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{E}[e^{tX}] 
  &= \int_0^{\infty}e^{tx}\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\mathrm{d}x\\
  &= \lambda \int_0^{\infty} e^{-(\lambda-t)x}\mathrm{d}x\\
  &= \frac{\lambda}{\lambda -t}\end{align}
  $$
  
-  计算 $-Y$ 的矩母函数:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{E}[e^{-tY}]
  &= \int_0^{\infty}
  e^{-ty}\mu e^{-\mu y}\mathrm{d}y\\
  &= \mu\int_0^{\infty}
  e^{-(\mu+t) y}\mathrm{d}y\\  
  &= \frac{\mu}{\mu+t}\end{align}
  $$
  
- 计算 $W= X-Y$ 的矩母函数:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{E}[e^{tW}]
  &= \text{E}[e^{t(X-Y)}]\\
  &= \text{E}[e^{tX}]\cdot \text{E}[e^{-tY}]\\
  &= \frac{\lambda}{\lambda - t}\cdot \frac{\mu}{\mu+t}\\
  &= \frac{\lambda \mu}{(\lambda-t)(\mu+t)}\end{align}
  $$
  
- 计算 $Z=\begin{cases}
  X,&\text{if }I=1\\
  -Y,&\text{if }I=0\end{cases}$ 的矩母函数:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{E}[e^{tZ}] 
  &= p\text{E}[e^{tX}] + (1-p)\text{E}[e^{-tY}]\\
  &= \frac{\mu}{\lambda+\mu}\frac{\lambda}{\lambda-t}+\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+\mu}\frac{\mu}{\mu+t}\\
  &= \frac{\lambda\mu}{(\lambda-t)(\mu+t)}\end{align}
  $$

综上所述，对于任意 $t$ (范围待确定，但这不重要) 都有 $\text{E}[e^{tW}] = \text{E}[e^{tZ}]$   
由于矩母函数唯一确定分布，因此 $W\overset{d}=Z$.

****

**(b) Give a simple explanation of the result in part (a)   
using the memoryless property of exponential distribution.**  
(5 marks) 

- **Hint:**  
  Consider $X$ and $Y$ as the waiting time for the occurrence of type (Ⅰ) and type (Ⅱ) events   
  and $I$ as the indicator function for the event $X>Y$.


**Solution:**    

- **指数分布的无记忆性: ** 
  对于指数随机变量 $X\sim \exp(\lambda)$，  
  有 $\text{P}\{X>t+s|X>s\} = \text{P}\{X>t\}\ \ (\forall\ 0<s<t)$ 成立.
  
- **考虑第一种情况: **  
  (Ⅰ) 类事件晚于 (Ⅱ) 类事件发生，  
  即事件 $\{X>Y\}$ 发生，也即事件 $\{I=1\}$ 发生.  
  对于任意 $s>0$ 都有:    
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{P}\{Z>s\}
  &= \text{P}\{I=1\}\cdot \text{P}\{X>s\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{X>Y\}\cdot \text{P}\{X>s\}\quad (应用指数分布的无记忆性)\\
  &= \text{P}\{X>Y\}\cdot \text{P}\{X>Y+s|X>Y\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{X>Y+s\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{X-Y>s\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{W>s\}\end{align}
  $$
  也就是说，对于任意 $s>0$ 都有 $\text{P}\{Z\leq s\}=\text{P}\{W\leq s\}$
  
- **考虑第二种情况: **  
  (Ⅰ) 类事件早于 (Ⅱ) 类事件发生，或同时发生，  
  即事件 $\{X\leq Y\}$ 发生，也即事件 $\{I=0\}$ 发生.  
  对于任意 $s\leq 0$ 都有:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{P}\{Z\leq s\}
  &= \text{P}\{I=0\}\cdot \text{P}\{-Y\leq s\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{X\leq Y\}\cdot \text{P}\{Y\geq -s\}\quad (应用指数分布的无记忆性)\\
  &= \text{P}\{Y\geq X\}\cdot \text{P}\{Y\geq X-s|Y\geq X\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{Y\geq X-s\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{X-Y\leq s\}\\
  &= \text{P}\{W\leq s\}\end{align}
  $$

综上所述，我们对于任意 $s\in\mathbb R$ 都有 $\text{P}\{Z\leq s\}=\text{P}\{W\leq s\}$ 成立，  
由于累计分布函数 (CDF) 唯一确定分布，因此 $W\overset{d}=Z$.



## Problem 2

Let $\{N(t):t\geq 0\}$ be a Poisson process with rate $\lambda>0$   
that is independent of the non-negative random variable $T$ with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$.  
Find:  

**(a) $\text{Cov}(T,N(T))$**    
(5 marks) 

- **Lemma: (Poisson 过程的结论)**  
  $N(t)\sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda t)$   
  因此有 $\begin{cases}
  \text{E}[N(t)]=\lambda t\\
  \text{Var}[N(t)]=\lambda t\\
  \text{E}[(N(t))^2] = \lambda t+ \lambda^2t^2\end{cases}$

**Solution: (全期望公式)**  

- 计算 $\text{E}[N(T)]$:  
  $\begin{align}
  \text{E}[N(T)]
  &= \text{E}[\text{E}[N(T)|T]]\\
  &= \text{E}[\lambda T]\\
  &= \lambda \mu\end{align}$
- 计算 $\text{E}[TN(T)]$:   
  $\begin{align}
  \text{E}[TN(T)]
  &= 
  \text{E}[\text{E}[TN(T)|T]]\\
  &=
  \text{E}[T\cdot \lambda T]\\
  &=
  \lambda \text{E}[T^2]\\
  &=
  \lambda [\text{Var}(T)+(\text{E}[T])^2]\\
  &=
  \lambda (\sigma^2 + \mu^2)\end{align}$
- 计算 $\text{Cov}(T,N(T))$:  
  $\begin{align}
  \text{E}[T,N(T)]
  &= \text{E}[TN(T)]-\text{E}[T]\cdot\text{E}[N(T)]\\
  &= \lambda (\sigma^2+\mu^2) - \mu\cdot \lambda\mu\\
  &= \lambda \sigma^2\end{align}$ 

***

**(b) $\text{Var}(N(T))$**    
(5 marks) 

- **Solution 1: (全方差公式)**    
  $\begin{align}
  \text{Var}(N(T))
  &= \text{E}[\text{Var}[N(T)|T]] + \text{Var}[\text{E}[N(T)|T]]\\
  &= \text{E}[\lambda T] + \text{Var}[\lambda T]\\
  &= \lambda\mu + \lambda ^2\sigma^2\end{align}$
- **Solution 2: **  
  - 计算 $\text{E}[(N(T))^2]$:  
    $\begin{align}
    \text{E}[(N(T))^2]
    &= 
    \text{E}[\text{E}[(N(T))^2|T]]\\
    &=
    \text{E}[\lambda T+\lambda ^2T^2]\\
    &=
    \lambda \text{E}[T]+\lambda^2\text{E}[T^2]\\
    &=
    \lambda\mu + \lambda^2 (\mu^2+\sigma^2)\\ 
    \end{align}$ 
  - 计算 $\text{Var}(N(T))$:  
    $\begin{align}
    \text{Var}(N(T))
    &= 
    \text{E}[(N(T))^2]-(\text{E}[N(T)])^2\\
    &=
    \lambda\mu+\lambda^2(\mu^2+\sigma^2) - (\lambda \mu)^2\\
    &=
    \lambda\mu + \lambda^2\sigma^2\end{align}$ 



## Problem 3

Let $Y_1,Y_2,\dots$ be independent and identically distributed random variables   
with cdf $F(y) = y^\alpha$, for all $0<y<1$ and $\alpha>0$.  
Let $\{N(t):t\geq 0\}$ be a Poisson process with rate $\lambda>0$, independent of $\{Y_i\}$.  
Determine $\text{P}\{Z(t)>z|N(t)>0\}$ where $Z(t)=\min\{Y_1,Y_2,\dots,Y_{N(t)}\}$.  
(10 marks) 

**Solution:**    
$$
F(y) = \begin{cases}
0,&\text{if }\ y\leq 0\\
y^\alpha, &\text{if }\ 0<y<1\\
1, &\text{if }\ y=1\end{cases}
$$
首先对 $N(t)$ 取条件 $N(t) = n\ (n\geq 1)$，则此时 $Z(t) = Y_{(1)}$  
对于任意 $z\in\mathbb R$，我们有:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{Z(t)>z|N(t)=n\}
&= \text{P}\{Y_{(1)}>z|N(t)=n\}\\
&= \text{P}\{Y_{1}>z,\dots,Y_{N(t)}>z|N(t)=n\}\\
&= \text{P}\{Y_1>z,\dots,Y_n>z\}\\
&= \text{P}\{Y_1>z\}\dotsm \text{P}\{Y_n>z\}\\
&= (1-F(z))^n\\
&= \begin{cases}
1,&\text{if }\ z\leq 0\\
(1-z^\alpha)^n, &\text{if }\ 0< z<1\\
0,&\text{if }\ z\geq 1\end{cases}\end{align}
$$
因此对于任意 $z\in\mathbb R$，我们有:    
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{Z(t)>z|N(t)>0\}
&= 
\frac{\text{P}\{Z(t)>z,N(t)>0\}}{\text{P}\{N(t)>0\}}\\
&=
\frac{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
\text{P}\{Z(t)>z|N(t)=n\}\text{P}\{N(t)=n\}}
{1-\text{P}\{N(t)=0\}}\\
&=
\frac{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
\{(1-F(z))^n\cdot e^{-\lambda t}\frac{(\lambda t)^n}{n!}\}}{1-e^{-\lambda t}\frac{(\lambda t)^0}{0!}}\\
&=
\frac{e^{-\lambda t}}{1-e^{-\lambda t}} \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{((1-F(z))\lambda t)^n}{n!}\\
&=
\frac{e^{(1-F(z))\lambda t}-1}{e^{\lambda t}-1}\\
&=
\begin{cases}
1,&\text{if }\ z\leq 0\\
\frac{e^{(1-z^\alpha) \lambda t}-1}{e^{\lambda t}-1}, &\text{if }\ 0< z<1\\
0,&\text{if }\ z\geq 1\end{cases}\end{align}
$$




## Problem 4

Suppose $\mathbf N=\{N(t):t\geq 0\}$ is a Poisson process with rate $\lambda$   
and $\{T_1,T_2,\dots\}$ is the sequence of arrival time.  
Let $f:\mathbb R_+\to\mathbb R$, find the value of $\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}f(T_i)]$.  
(15 marks) 

- **Lemma:**   
  **(到达时刻的联合条件分布, Introduction to Probability Models 定理 $5.2$)**  
  假设 $\mathbf N = \{N(t):t\geq 0\}$ 是参数为 $\lambda$ 的 Poisson 过程，记 $T_1,T_2,\dots$ 为到达时刻.  
  给定 $t>0$，则有 $(T_1,T_2,\dots,T_n|N(t)=n)\overset{d}= (U_{(1)},U_{(2)},\dots,U_{(n)})$   
  其中 $U_{(1)},U_{(2)},\dots,U_{(n)}$ 为对应于 $U_1,U_2,\dots,U_n\overset{iid}\sim \text{Uniform}(0,t)$ 的次序统计量.  
  **上面的结论通常可表述为: **  
  在 $(0,t)$ 中已经发生了 $n$ 个事件的条件下，  
  事件发生的时间 $T_1,T_2,\dots,T_n$ (考虑为无次序的随机变量时) 是在 $(0,t)$ 上独立均匀地分布的.  
  

**Solution:**  
$$
\begin{align}
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}f(T_i)]
&= 
\text{E}[\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}f(T_i)|\mathbf N]]\quad (应用引理)\\
&=  
\text{E}[\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}f(U_{(i)})|\mathbf N]]\\
&=
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} \text{E}[f(U_{(i)})|\mathbf N]]\\
&=
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} \text{E}[f(U_{(i)})]]\\
&=
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} \text{E}[f(U_{i})]]\qquad (去掉次序)\end{align}
$$

其中 $U_{(1)},\dots,U_{(N(t))}$ 为 $U_1,\dots,U_{N(t)}\overset{iid}\sim \text{Uniform}(0,t)$ 对应的次序统计量.  

记 $C=\text{E}[f(U_i)] = \int_0^t f(u)\cdot \frac{1}{t}du = \frac{1}{t}\int_0^t f(u)du$   
则有:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)}f(T_i)]
&= \text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} \text{E}[f(U_{i})]]\\
&= \text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} C]\\
&= C \text{E}[N(t)]\\ 
&= C \cdot \lambda t\\
&= \frac{1}{t}\int_0^t f(u)du \cdot \lambda t\\
&= \lambda \int_0^t f(u)du\end{align}
$$


## Problem 5

Cars pass a junction on the highway at a Poisson rate of one per minute.  
Suppose that each car has $0.05$ probability of speeding.

**(a) Find the probability that more than one car are speeding through this junction in an hour.** 
(5 marks) 

- **Lemma: (Poisson 过程的拆分)**  
  在之前的假设下，记 $N_i(t)\ (i=1,\dots,k)$ 为到时刻 $t$ 为止类型 $i$ 事件发生的个数，  
  则 $N_i(t)\ (i=1,\dots,k)$ 是具有均值 $\text{E}[N_i(t)] = \lambda \int_0^t p_i(t) ds$ 的**独立 Poisson 随机变量**，  
  即 $N_i(t) \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda \int_0^t p_i(t) ds)$ 且它们相互独立.  
  这样我们就拆分得到了一系列非齐次 Poisson 过程 $\{N_i(t):t\geq 0\}$..
  - **推论: **  
    特殊地，若 $p_1(\cdot),p_2(\cdot),\dots,p_k(\cdot)$ 都是常数函数，  
    则对于任意 $i=1,2,\dots,k$ 我们有 $N_i(t) = \text{Poisson}(\lambda p_i)$ 且它们相互独立，  
    即 $\{N_i(t):t\geq 0\}$ 是参数为 $\lambda p_i$ 的独立 Poisson 过程.

**Solution:**  
记 $\begin{cases}
\lambda = 1\\ 
p = 0.05\end{cases}$      
分别记加速和不加速的车辆为 (Ⅰ) 型和 (Ⅱ) 型，  
记其计数过程为 $\mathbf N_1 =  \{N_1(t):t\geq 0\}$ 和 $\mathbf N_2 =  \{N_2(t):t\geq 0\}$.  
根据引理可知，  
$\mathbf N_1$ 和 $\mathbf N_2$ 分别是速率为 $\lambda p = 0.05$ 和 $\lambda(1-p) = 0.95$ 的 Poisson 过程，且相互独立.  

记 $t = 60\ (\text{min})$   
则 $N_1(t) \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda p t) = \text{Poisson}(3)$  
于是 $(0,t)$ 时间段内加速车辆数超过 $1$ 的概率为:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{N_1(t)\geq 2\}
&=
1- \text{P}\{N_1(t)=0\} - \text{P}\{N_1(t)=1\}\\
&=
1- e^{-3}\frac{3^0}{0!} - e^{-3}\frac{3^1}{1!}\\
&=
1-4e^{-3}\\
&\approx
0.801\end{align}
$$

***

**(b) Given that ten cars have been speeding through the junction in an hour,   
what is the expected number of cars that had passed the junction?**  
(5 marks)

**Solution:**  
记 $t = 60\ (\text{min})$    
我们知道 $\begin{cases}
N(t) \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda t) = \text{Poisson}(60)\\
N_1(t) \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda p t) = \text{Poisson}(3)\\
N_2(t) \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda (1-p) t) = \text{Poisson}(57)\end{cases}$  
于是我们有:
$$
\begin{align}
\text{E}[N(t)|N_1(t)=10]
&=
\text{E}[N_1(t)+N_2(t)|N_1(t) = 10]\quad (\mathbf N_1\ \bot\ \mathbf N_2)\\
&=
10 + \text{E}[N_2(t)]\\
&=
10 + 57\\
&=
67\end{align}
$$

***

**(c) If 50 cars have passed the junction in an hour,   
what is the probability that 5 of them were speeding?**  
(5 marks)

**Solution:**  
记 $t = 60\ (\text{min})$  
我们首先计算给定 $N(t)=n$ 条件下 $N_1(t)$ 的分布:   
对于任意 $k=0,1,\dots,n$ 都有:    
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{N_1(t)=k|N(t)=n\}
&=
\frac{\text{P}\{N_1(t)=k,N_2(t)=n-k\}}{\text{P}\{N(t)=n\}}\\
&=
\frac{\text{P}\{N_1(t)=k\}\cdot\text{P}\{N_2(t)=n-k\}}{\text{P}\{N(t)=n\}}\\
&=
\frac{e^{-\lambda p t}\frac{(\lambda p t)^k}{k!}\cdot 
e^{-\lambda (1-p) t}\frac{(\lambda (1-p) t)^{n-k}}{(n-k)!}}{e^{-\lambda t}\frac{(\lambda t)^n}{n!}}\\
&=
\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}\\
&=
\text{P}\{B(n,p)=k\}\end{align}
$$
说明 $(N_1(t)|N(t)=n)\overset{d}= B(n,p)$   
于是我们有:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{N_1(t)=5|N(t)=50\}
&= \text{P}\{B(50,0.05) = 5\}\\
&= \binom{50}{5}(0.05)^5 (1-0.05)^{45}\\
&\approx 0.0658\end{align}
$$


## Problem 6

Let $\{X(t):t\geq 0\}$ be a compound Poisson process:  
$X(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} Y_i$ with Poisson rate $\lambda = 1$   
and $Y_1,Y_2,\dots$ are $\text{i.i.d.}$ random variables distributed according to $\begin{cases}
\text{P}\{Y=1\} = \frac13\\
\text{P}\{Y=2\} = \frac23\end{cases}$   
Calculate $\text{P}\{X(2)=3\}$.  
(10 marks)

**Solution:**  
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{X(2) = 3\}
&=
\text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{N(2)}\sum} Y_i = 3\}\\
&= 
\underset{n=0}{\overset{\infty}\sum}
\text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{N(2)}\sum} Y_i = 3|N(2) = n\}
\text{P}\{N(2) = n\}\quad (\{Y_i\}\ \bot\ N(t))\\
&=\underset{n=0}{\overset{\infty}\sum}
\text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{n}\sum} Y_i = 3\}
\text{P}\{N(2) = n\}\end{align}
$$
注意到当 $n=0,1$ 或 $n\geq 4$ 时都有 $\text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{n}\sum} Y_i = 3\} = 0$ 成立   
(基于题干信息 $\begin{cases}
\text{P}\{Y=1\} = \frac13\\
\text{P}\{Y=2\} = \frac23\end{cases}$)  
因此我们有:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{X(2) = 3\}
&= \underset{n=0}{\overset{\infty}\sum}
\text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{n}\sum} Y_i = 3\}
\text{P}\{N(2) = n\}\\
&= 0 + \text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{2}\sum} Y_i = 3\}
\text{P}\{N(2) = 2\} + \text{P}\{\underset{i=1}{\overset{3}\sum} Y_i = 3\}
\text{P}\{N(2) = 3\} + 0\\
&=
\binom{2}{1} 
\text{P}\{Y_1=1\}\text{P}\{Y_2=2\}\text{P}\{\text{Poisson}(2)=2\}+
\binom{3}{3} 
(\text{P}\{Y_1=1\})^3\text{P}\{\text{Poisson}(2)=3\}\\
&=
2\cdot \frac13 \cdot \frac23 \cdot e^{-2}\frac{2^2}{2!}+
1\cdot (\frac13)^3 \cdot e^{-2}\frac{2^3}{3!}\\
&=
\frac{8}{9}e^{-2}+\frac{4}{81}e^{-2}\\
&=
\frac{76}{81}e^{-1}\\
&\approx
0.127\end{align}
$$


## Problem 7

A pedestrian wants to cross a road at a spot where cars go by   
in accordance with a Poisson process with rate $\lambda$.  
He/She will begin to cross the first time he/she sees that   
there will be no any cars passing for the next $c$ time units.  
(Assume that he/she is able to forsee that.)  

Let $N$ denote the number of cars that pass before he finally crosses the road.  
Let $R$ denote the time at which he finally starts to cross the road.  
Let $\{X_i\}$​ denote the inter-arrival times of cars (i.e. waiting times of Poisson Process)

**(a) Determine the distribution of $N$ and calculate $\text{E}[N]$​.**  
(5 marks)  

- **Lemma 1:**  
  计算指数随机变量 $X\sim \exp(\lambda)$ 的 $\text{CDF}$:  
  $$
  \begin{align}
  F(x)
  &= \int_0^x \lambda e^{-\lambda t}dt\\
  &= \int_0^{\lambda x} e^{-u}du\\
  &= -e^{-u}|^{\lambda x}_0\\
  &= -e^{-\lambda x} + e^0\\
  &= 1 - e^{-\lambda x}\end{align}
  $$
  
- **Lemma 2:**  
  计算几何随机变量 $X\sim \text{Geo}(p)$ 的均值:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  \text{E}(X) &= \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} i\cdot \text{pmf}(i) \\
  &=  \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} i\cdot (1-p)^{i-1}p \\
  &= p\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} i\cdot q^{i-1}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ (q:= 1-p) \\
  &= p\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}(q^i) \\
  &= p \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}q^i\right)\\
  &= p\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}\left(\frac{q}{1-q}\right) \\
  &= \frac{p}{(1-q)^2} \\
  &= \frac1p
  \end{align}
  $$

**Solution:**    
$N$ 相当于首次等待时间超过 $c$ 的车辆出现之前，已经经过的车辆数.  
我们知道 $\{X_i\}$ 作为 Poisson 过程的等待时间，有 $\{T_i\}\overset{iid}\sim \exp(\lambda)$ 成立.  
事件 $\{N=n\}$ 发生等价于事件 $\{T_1\leq c,\dots,T_n\leq c,T_{n+1}>c\}$ 发生.  
因此对于任意 $n = 0,1,\dots$ 我们有:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{N=n\}
&= 
\text{P}\{T_1\leq c,\dots,T_n\leq c,T_{n+1}>c\}\\
&= 
\text{P}\{T_1\leq c\}\dotsm \text{P}\{T_n\leq c\}\text{P}\{T_{n+1}>c\}\\
&=
(1-e^{-\lambda c})^n\cdot e^{-\lambda c}\end{align}
$$
说明 $N+1$ 服从成功概率为  $e^{-\lambda c}$ 的几何分布 $\text{Geo}(e^{-\lambda c})$.  
根据 Lemma 2 可知 $\text{E}[N+1] = \frac{1}{e^{-\lambda c}} = e^{\lambda c}$，  
故 $\text{E}[N] = e^{\lambda c}-1$.

***

**(b) Compute $\text{E}[X_i|X_i<c]$​​.**  
(10 marks)   
[probability - Crossing the road - Mathematics Stack Exchange](https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/1607241/crossing-the-road)

**Solution:**  
首先计算条件概率:   
对于任意 $0<x<c$ 都有:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{P}\{X_i=x|X_i<c\} 
&= \frac{\text{P}\{X_i=x,\ X_i<c\}}{\text{P}\{X_i<c\}}\\
&= \frac{\text{P}\{X=x\}}{\text{P}\{X_i<c\}}\\
&= \frac{\lambda e^{-\lambda x}}{1-e^{-\lambda c}}\end{align}
$$
现在计算条件期望:   
$$
\begin{align}
\text{E}[X_i|X_i<c]
&= 
\int_0^c x\cdot \text{P}\{X_i=x|X_i<c\}\mathrm{d}x\\
&=
\int_0^c x\cdot  \frac{\lambda e^{-\lambda x}}{1-e^{-\lambda c}}\mathrm{d}x\\
&=
\frac{1}{\lambda (1-e^{-\lambda c})}\int_0^{\lambda c} 
ue^{-u}du\\
&=
\frac{1}{\lambda (1-e^{-\lambda c})} 
(-1-u)e^{-u}|^{\lambda c}_0\\
&=
\frac{1}{\lambda (1-e^{-\lambda c})} 
[1-(\lambda c+1)e^{-\lambda c}]\\
&=
\frac{e^{\lambda c}-(\lambda c + 1)}{\lambda(e^{\lambda c}-1)}
\end{align}
$$

**(c) Express $R$ in terms of $N$ and $\{X_i\}$ and determine $\text{E}[R]$​​**  
(10 marks)

- **Hint:  **use the results in (b) 

**Solution:**     
我们有 $R = \sum_{i=1}^{N}  X_i$ 成立.
$$
\begin{align}
\text{E}[R]
&= 
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N}  X_i|X_1<c,\dots,X_N<c,X_{N+1}\geq c]\\
&=
\text{E}[\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N}  X_i|N,X_1<c,\dots,X_N<c,X_{N+1}\geq c]]\\
&=
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N}  \text{E}[X_i|X_i<c]]
\qquad(\{X_i\}\ 相互独立)\\
&= 
\text{E}[\sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{e^{\lambda c}-(\lambda c + 1)}{\lambda(e^{\lambda c}-1)}]
\quad (代入(\text{b})结论\text{E}[X_i|X_i<c]=\frac{e^{\lambda c}-(\lambda c + 1)}{\lambda(e^{\lambda c}-1)})\\
&=
\text{E}[N]\cdot \frac{e^{\lambda c}-(\lambda c + 1)}{\lambda(e^{\lambda c}-1)}
\quad (代入(\text{a})结论\text{E}[N] = e^{\lambda c}-1)\\
&=
(e^{\lambda c}-1)\cdot\frac{e^{\lambda c}-(\lambda c + 1)}{\lambda(e^{\lambda c}-1)}\\
&=
\frac{e^{\lambda c}-1}{\lambda} - c\end{align}
$$

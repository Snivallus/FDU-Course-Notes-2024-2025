## Problem 1

The coeffcients $\beta$ of a linear regression model, $Y=X\beta + \varepsilon$ , are estimated by $\hat \beta = (X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y$.   
Then the associated fitted values are given by $\hat Y = X\hat \beta = X(X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}Y = HY$,  
where $H=X(X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}$ is the hat matrix, which is a projection operator.  
Hence, linear regression projects the response $Y$ onto $\text{span}(X)$ (i.e., column space of design matrix)  
Consequently, the residuals $\hat \varepsilon$ and $\hat Y$ are orthogonal.  

Now consider the ridge estimator of the regression coeffcients: $\hat \beta(\lambda) = (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y$.  
Let $\hat Y(\lambda)=X\hat \beta(\lambda)$ be the vector of associated fitted values. 

- ① Show that the matrix $H(\lambda) = X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}$ is not a projection matrix (for any $\lambda>0$)
- ② Show that the ridge fit $\hat Y(\lambda)$ is not orthogonal to the associated ridge residuals $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)$ (for any $\lambda >0$)
- ③ Given that $\varepsilon\sim N(0_n,\sigma^2 I_n)$, derive the distribution of ridge residuals $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)$

### Part (1)

Show that the matrix $H(\lambda) = X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}$ is not a projection matrix (for any $\lambda>0$)

**Proof:**  
根据泛函分析中的结论:  
Hilbert 空间 $(V,\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle)$ 中的线性算子 $H$ 是投影算子当且仅当 $H$ 是幂等且自伴的，即满足 $\begin{cases}
H^2=H\\
H^*=H\end{cases}$   
特别地，有限维内积空间一定是 Hilbert 空间.   
复 Euclid 空间上的伴随算子 $H^*$ 的表示矩阵即为算子 $H$ 表示矩阵的共轭转置.  

考虑到 $H(\lambda) = X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}$ 一定是对称阵，  
故我们要说明它不是投影算子，只需说明它不是幂等算子即可，  
即不满足 $(H(\lambda))^2 = H(\lambda)$，也即其存在某个特征值不是 $0$ 或 $1$.

****

设 $X\in \mathbb R^{n\times (p+1)}$ 的奇异值分解为 $X=U\Sigma V^{\mathrm T}$  
其中 $U\in \mathbb R^{n\times n},V\in \mathbb R^{(p+1)\times (p+1)}$ 是实正交阵，  
而 $\Sigma\in \mathbb R^{n\times (p+1)}$ 的对角元 $\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_{p+1}$ 均为正实数 (因为模型的列满秩假设规定 $\rank(X)=p+1<n$)  
则对于任意给定的 $\lambda>0$，我们都有:  
$$
\begin{align}
H(\lambda)
&=
X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}\\
&=
U\Sigma V^{\mathrm T}[(U\Sigma V^{\mathrm T})^{\mathrm T}(U\Sigma V^{\mathrm T}) + \lambda I]^{-1} (U\Sigma V^{\mathrm T})^{\mathrm T}\\
&=
U\Sigma V^{\mathrm T}(V\Sigma^{\mathrm T}\Sigma V^{\mathrm T} + \lambda I)^{-1}V\Sigma^{\mathrm T}U^{\mathrm T}\\
&=
U\Sigma (\Sigma^{\mathrm T}\Sigma + \lambda I)^{-1}\Sigma^{\mathrm T} U^{\mathrm T}\\
&=
U
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \lambda}\\
& \ddots\\
& & \frac{\sigma_{p+1}^2}{\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda}\\
& & & 0\\
& & & & \ddots\\
& & & & & 0
\end{bmatrix}U^{\mathrm T}\\
&=
U\text{diag}\left\{\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \lambda},
\dots,
\frac{\sigma_{p+1}^2}{\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda},
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\}U^{\mathrm T}\\
\hline
(H(\lambda))^2 
&=
U\text{diag}\left\{
\left(\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \lambda}\right)^2,
\dots,
\left(\frac{\sigma_{p+1}^2}{\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda}\right)^2,
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\}U^{\mathrm T}
\end{align}
$$
显然对于任意 $\lambda>0$，$H(\lambda)$ 都有 $p+1$ 个特征值 $\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \lambda},
\dots,
\frac{\sigma_{p+1}^2}{\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda}$ 既不为 $0$，又不为 $1$  
因此 $H(\lambda)$ 不是幂等算子 (即不满足 $(H(\lambda))^2=H(\lambda)$)  
具体来说:  
$$
\begin{align}
&H(\lambda) - (H(\lambda))^2\\
&=
U\text{diag}\left\{\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \lambda},
\dots,
\frac{\sigma_{p+1}^2}{\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda},
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\}U^{\mathrm T}
-
U\text{diag}\left\{
\left(\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_1^2 + \lambda}\right)^2,
\dots,
\left(\frac{\sigma_{p+1}^2}{\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda}\right)^2,
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\}U^{\mathrm T} \\

&=
U\text{diag}\left\{
\frac{\lambda\sigma_1^2}{(\sigma_1^2 + \lambda)^2},
\dots,
\frac{\lambda\sigma_{p+1}^2}{(\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda)^2},
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\}U^{\mathrm T}\\
&\neq
0_{n\times n}
\end{align}
$$


### Part (2)

Show that the ridge fit $\hat y(\lambda)$ is not orthogonal to the associated ridge residuals $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)$ (for any $\lambda >0$)

**Proof:**   
对于任意 $\lambda>0$，我们都有:
$$
\begin{align}
(\hat y(\lambda))^{\mathrm T} \hat \varepsilon(\lambda)
&=
(\hat H(\lambda)y)^{\mathrm T} (y - \hat H(\lambda)y)\quad (\text{note that }(\hat H(\lambda))^{\mathrm T} = \hat H(\lambda))\\
&=
y^{\mathrm T} \hat H(\lambda) (I-\hat H(\lambda)) y\\
&=
y^{\mathrm T}(\hat H(\lambda) - (\hat H(\lambda))^2) y\\
&=
y^{\mathrm T} U\text{diag}\left\{
\frac{\lambda\sigma_1^2}{(\sigma_1^2 + \lambda)^2},
\dots,
\frac{\lambda\sigma_{p+1}^2}{(\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda)^2},
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\}U^{\mathrm T} y\\
&=
(U^{\mathrm T}y)^{\mathrm T} \text{diag}\left\{
\frac{\lambda\sigma_1^2}{(\sigma_1^2 + \lambda)^2},
\dots,
\frac{\lambda\sigma_{p+1}^2}{(\sigma_{p+1}^2 + \lambda)^2},
\underset{n-p-1}{\underbrace{0,\dots,0}}\right\} (U^{\mathrm T}y)
\end{align}
$$
记正交阵 $U$ 的列向量组为 $U=[u_1,\dots,u_n]\in \mathbb R^{n\times n}$  
显然 $(\hat y(\lambda))^{\mathrm T} \hat \varepsilon(\lambda) = 0$ 当且仅当 $U^{\mathrm T}y=[u_1^{\mathrm T}y,\dots,u_n^{\mathrm T}y]^{\mathrm T}$ 的前 $p+1$ 个分量全为零，  
即当且仅当 $y\in \text{span}\{u_1,\dots,u_{p+1}\}^\bot = \text{span}\{u_{p+1},\dots,u_{n}\}$    
即当且仅当 $y$ 正交于 $\text{span}(X)= \text{span}\{u_1,\dots,u_{p+1}\}$ 

通常来说，响应变量的观测向量 $y$ 不完全正交于设计矩阵 $X$ 列空间 $\text{span}(X)$，  
因此我们可以认为 $(\hat Y(\lambda))^{\mathrm T} \hat \varepsilon(\lambda) \neq 0\ (\forall\ \lambda>0)$   
即对于任意 $\lambda>0$，$\hat Y(\lambda)$ 和 $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)$ 都不是正交的.



### Part (3)

Given that $\varepsilon\sim N(0_n,\sigma^2 I_n)$, derive the distribution of ridge residuals $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)$

**Solution:**  
对于任意 $\lambda>0$，我们都有:  
$$
\begin{align}
\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)
&=
y - \hat y(\lambda)\\
&=
y - H(\lambda) y\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda))y\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda)) (X\beta + \varepsilon)\\
\end{align}
$$
根据 $\varepsilon\sim N(0_n,\sigma^2 I_n)$ 可知 $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)$ 也服从多元正态分布，  
且其均值向量和协方差矩阵为:  
$$
\begin{align}
\text{E}[\hat\varepsilon(\lambda)]
&=
\text{E}[(I-H(\lambda)) (X\beta + \varepsilon)]\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda))X\beta  +  (I-H(\lambda))\text{E}[\varepsilon]\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda))X\beta  +  (I-H(\lambda))\cdot 0_n\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda))X\beta \\

\hline
\text{Cov}(\hat \varepsilon(\lambda))
&=
\text{Cov}[(I-H(\lambda))(X\beta+ \varepsilon)]\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda))\cdot \text{Cov}(\varepsilon) \cdot [(I-H(\lambda))]^{\mathrm T}\quad (\text{note that }(H(\lambda))^{\mathrm T} = H(\lambda))\\
&=
(I-H(\lambda))\cdot \sigma^2 I_n \cdot (I-H(\lambda))\\
&=
\sigma^2 (I-H(\lambda))^2
\end{align}
$$
因此 $\hat \varepsilon(\lambda)\sim N((I-H(\lambda))X\beta, \sigma^2 (I-H(\lambda))^2)$



## Problem 2

Recall that there exists a $\lambda>0$ such that $\text{MSE}(\hat \beta)>\text{MSE}(\hat \beta(\lambda))$   
(where $\hat \beta = (X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y$ and $\hat \beta(\lambda) = (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y$)  
Verify that this carries to the fitted value, that is, there exists a $\lambda>0$ such that $\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta)>\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta(\lambda))$ 

**Proof:**     
考虑 $X\hat \beta(\lambda)=H(\lambda)y$ 的均方误差:  
(其中 $H(\lambda) = X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}$)  
$$
\begin{align}
\text{MSE}[X\hat \beta(\lambda)]
&=
\text{MSE}[H(\lambda)y]\\
&=
\text{E}[\|H(\lambda)y - X\beta\|^2]\\
&=
\text{E}[\|H(\lambda)y - \text{E}[H(\lambda)y] +\text{E}[H(\lambda)y] - X\beta\|^2]\\
&=
\text{E}[\|H(\lambda)y - H(\lambda)\text{E}[y]\|^2] 
+
\|H(\lambda)\text{E}[y] - X\beta\|^2\\
&=
\tr{(\text{Cov}(H(\lambda)y))}  + \text{bias}^2(H(\lambda)y)\\
&=
\tr{(H(\lambda) \text{Cov}(y)H(\lambda)^{\mathrm T})} + \|H(\lambda) X\beta - X\beta\|^2\\
&=
\tr{(H(\lambda)\cdot \sigma^2 I_n\cdot H(\lambda)^{\mathrm T})} + \|H(\lambda) X\beta - X\beta\|^2\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{
X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T} X (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}
\}
+
\|X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X\beta - X\beta\|^2\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{
(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T} X (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X
\}
+
\|-\lambda X(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}\beta\|^2\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{
(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T} X (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X
\}
+
\lambda^2 \beta^{\mathrm T} (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}\beta\\
\end{align}
$$
设 $X^{\mathrm T}X\in \mathbb R^{(p+1)\times (p+1)}$ 的谱分解为 $X^{\mathrm T}X = UDU^{\mathrm T}$  
其中 $U\in \mathbb R^{(p+1)\times (p+1)}$ 为正交阵，$D=\text{diag}\{d_1,\dots,d_{p+1}\}$ 为对角阵.  
于是我们有:  
$$
\begin{align}
&\text{MSE}[X\hat \beta(\lambda)]\\
&=\sigma^2 \tr\{
(X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T} X (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X
\}
+
\lambda^2 \beta^{\mathrm T} (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X (X^{\mathrm T}X+\lambda I)^{-1}\beta\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{(UDU^{\mathrm T} + \lambda I)^{-1} UDU^{\mathrm T} (UDU^{\mathrm T} + \lambda I)^{-1} UDU^{\mathrm T}\}
+
\lambda^2 \beta^{\mathrm T}
(UDU^{\mathrm T} + \lambda I)^{-1}
UDU^{\mathrm T}
(UDU^{\mathrm T} + \lambda I)^{-1}\beta\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{U(D+\lambda I)^{-1} D(D+\lambda I)^{-1} D U^{\mathrm T}\} +\lambda^2 \beta^{\mathrm T} U(D+\lambda I)^{-1} D(D+\lambda I)^{-1}U^{\mathrm T}\beta\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr((D+\lambda I)^{-2} D^2) + \lambda^2 \beta^{\mathrm T} U (D+\lambda I)^{-2}D U^{\mathrm T}\beta\\
\end{align}
$$
注意到最小二乘估计量 $\hat \beta = (X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y=\hat \beta(0)$，即 Ridge 估计量 $\lambda=0$ 的情形.  
因此要证明存在某个 $\lambda>0$ 使得 $\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta(\lambda))<\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta)$，  
我们只要证明 $\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta(\lambda))$ 关于 $\lambda$ 的导数在 $\lambda=0$ 处为负值即可.
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{d\lambda}\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta(\lambda))
&=
\frac{d}{d\lambda} 
\left\{
\sigma^2 \tr((D+\lambda I)^{-2} D^2) + \lambda^2 \beta^{\mathrm T} U (D+\lambda I)^{-2}D U^{\mathrm T}\beta
\right\}\\
&=
-2\sigma^2\tr((D+\lambda I)^{-3}D^2) + 2\lambda \beta^{\mathrm T} U (D+\lambda I)^{-2}D U^{\mathrm T}\beta
+
\lambda^2 \cdot (-2 \beta^{\mathrm T} U (D+\lambda I)^{-3}D U^{\mathrm T}\beta)\\

\hline
\frac{d}{d\lambda}\text{MSE}(X\hat \beta(\lambda)){\LARGE|}_{\lambda=0}
&=
-2\sigma^2 \tr(D^{-3}D^2) + 0 + 0\\
&=
-2\sigma^2 \tr(D^{-1})\\
&<0
\end{align}
$$
命题得证.



## Problem 3

Consider an ridge estimator for $\beta\in \mathbb R^{p+1}$ in linear regression model:  
$$
\hat\beta (\Lambda) = (X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y
$$
where $U\in \mathbb R^{(p+1)\times (p+1)}$ is an orthogonal matrix such that $U^{\mathrm T}X^{\mathrm T}X U = D=\text{diag}\{d_1,\dots,d_{p+1}\}$  
and $\Lambda=\text{diag}\{\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_{p+1}\}$ is a diagonal matrix.  
Prove that there exists $\Lambda\succ 0$ such that $\text{MSE}(\hat \beta(\Lambda))<\text{MSE}(\hat \beta)$ (where $\hat \beta = (X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y$)

**Proof:**  
考虑 $\hat\beta (\Lambda) = (X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y$ 的均方误差: 
$$
\begin{align}
&
\text{MSE}[\hat \beta(\Lambda)]\\
&=
\text{E}[\|\hat \beta(\Lambda) - \beta\|^2]\\
&=
\text{E}[\|\hat \beta(\Lambda) - \text{E}[\hat \beta(\Lambda)] +\text{E}[\hat \beta(\Lambda)] - \beta\|^2]\\
&=
\text{E}[\|\hat \beta(\Lambda) - \text{E}[\hat \beta(\Lambda)]\|^2] 
+
\|\text{E}[\hat \beta(\Lambda)] - \beta\|^2\\
&=
\tr{(\text{Cov}(\hat \beta(\Lambda)))}  + \text{bias}^2(\hat \beta(\Lambda))
\quad (\text{note that }\hat\beta (\Lambda) = (X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y)\\
&=
\tr\{(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}\cdot \text{Cov}(y)\cdot 
[(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}]^{\mathrm T}\} + 
\|(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}\text{E}[y] - \beta\|^2\\
&=
\tr\{(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}\cdot \sigma^2 I_n\cdot 
[(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}]^{\mathrm T}\} + 
\|(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T} X\beta - \beta\|^2\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}X (X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}\} + 
\|-(X^{\mathrm T}X+U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-1}U \Lambda U^{\mathrm T}\beta\|^2\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{(X^{\mathrm T}X+U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-2}X^{\mathrm T}X\} 
+
\beta^{\mathrm T} U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T}
(X^{\mathrm T}X+U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-2} U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T}\beta\quad (\text{note that }X^{\mathrm T}X=UDU^{\mathrm T})\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{(UDU^{\mathrm T} + U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-2}UDU^{\mathrm T}\}
+
\beta^{\mathrm T} U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T} (UDU^{\mathrm T} + U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T})^{-2} U\Lambda U^{\mathrm T}\beta\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{U(D+\Lambda)^{-2} DU^{\mathrm T}\} + \beta^{\mathrm T}U \Lambda (D+\Lambda)^{-2}\Lambda U^{\mathrm T}\beta\\
&=
\sigma^2 \tr\{(D+\Lambda)^{-2} D\} + \beta^{\mathrm T}U (D+\Lambda)^{-2}\Lambda^2 U^{\mathrm T}\beta\\
\end{align}
$$
注意到最小二乘估计量 $\hat \beta = (X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}X^{\mathrm T}y=\hat \beta(0_{(p+1)\times (p+1)})$，即 Ridge 估计量 $\Lambda=0_{(p+1)\times (p+1)}$ 的情形.  
因此要证明存在某个 $\Lambda\succ 0$ 使得 $\text{MSE}(\hat \beta(\Lambda))<\text{MSE}(\hat \beta)$，  
我们只要证明 $\text{MSE}(\hat \beta(\Lambda))$ 关于 $\Lambda$ 的梯度在 $\Lambda=0_{(p+1)\times (p+1)}$ 处为负定矩阵即可.  
$$
\begin{align}
\nabla_\Lambda \text{MSE}(\hat \beta (\Lambda))
&=
\nabla_\Lambda \{\sigma^2 \tr((D+\Lambda)^{-2} D) + \beta^{\mathrm T}U (D+\Lambda)^{-2}\Lambda^2 U^{\mathrm T}\beta\}\\
&=
-2\sigma^2 (D+\Lambda)^{-3}D
+
[(D+\Lambda)^{-2}\cdot 2\Lambda
+
(-2(D+\Lambda)^{-3})\Lambda^2]\cdot\text{diag}\{(U^{\mathrm T}\beta)\odot (U^{\mathrm T}\beta)\}\\
&=
-2\sigma^2(D+\Lambda)^{-3}D + 2(D+\Lambda)^{-2}\Lambda [I - (D+\Lambda)^{-1}\Lambda]
\cdot\text{diag}\{(U^{\mathrm T}\beta)\odot (U^{\mathrm T}\beta)\}\\

\hline
\nabla_\Lambda \text{MSE}(\hat \beta(\Lambda)){\LARGE|}_{\Lambda = 0_{(p+1)\times (p+1)}}
&=
-2\sigma^2 D^{-3}D + 0_{(p+1)\times (p+1)}\\
&=
-2\sigma^2 D^{-2}\\
&\prec 0
\end{align}
$$
命题得证.



## Problem 4

Suppose that only two values, $x=0,1$ are observed.  
For $x=0$, there are $10$ successes in $10$ trials.  
For $x=1$, there are $5$ successes in $10$ trials.  
Show that the logistic regression MLEs $\hat \alpha$, $\hat \beta$ does not exist.

**Solution:**    
更改记号: 将 $\alpha$ 改为 $\beta_0$，将 $\beta$ 改为 $\beta_1$，记 $\beta = [\beta_0,\beta_1]^{\mathrm T}$   
记样本为:  
$$
(x_i,y_i) = 
\begin{cases}
(0, 1) & \text{if }i=1,\dots,10\\
(1, 1) & \text{if }i=11,\dots,15\\
(1, 0) & \text{if }i=16,\dots,20 
\end{cases}
$$
因此设计矩阵和响应变量观测可以表示为:  
$$
X = 
\begin{bmatrix}
1_{10} & 0_{10}\\
1_{10} & 1_{10}
\end{bmatrix}
\quad
y=
\begin{bmatrix}
1_{15}\\
0_{5}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

***

记 $n=20$  
假设 $Y_i\ (i=1,\dots,n)$ 服从以下 Bernoulli 分布:  
$$
Y_i \sim \text{B}(1, p_i)\text{ i.e. }
Y_i =
\begin{cases}
1, & p_i\\
0, & 1-p_i
\end{cases}\\
\text{where }p_i := \text{P}\{Y=1|x_i\}
=
\sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)
$$
因此 $Y = [Y_1,\dots,Y_n]^{\mathrm T}$ 的联合概率密度函数为:  
$$
\begin{align}
f(Y) 
&:= \prod_{i=1}^n p_i^{Y_i}(1-p_i)^{1-Y_i}\\
&=
\prod_{i=1}^n (\sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i))^{Y_i} (1-\sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i))^{1-Y_i}\\
\end{align}
$$
对数似然函数为:  
$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal L(y|X,\beta)
&=
\log({\prod_{i=1}^n p_i^{y_i} (1-p_i)^{1-y_i}})\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{
y_i \log(p_i) + (1-y_i) \log(1-p_i) 
\right\}\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{
y_i \log({\sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)}) + (1-y_i)\log({1-\sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)})
\right\}\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{
y_i \log(\sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)) + (1-y_i)\log({\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)})
\right\}\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{
y_i \log(\frac{1}{1+\exp(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)}) 
-y_i\log (\frac{1}{1+\exp(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)})
+ \log({\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)})
\right\}\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n 
\left\{
y_i \log(\frac{1+\exp(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)}{1+\exp(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)})
+
\log({\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)})
\right\}\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{y_i\log({\exp(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)}) +\log(\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)))\right\}\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{y_i (\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i) +\log({\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)}) \right\}\\
&=
y^{\mathrm T}X\beta + 1_n^{\mathrm T}\log(\sigma(-X\beta))
\end{align}
$$
其关于 $\beta$ 的梯度为:  
$$
\begin{align}
\nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta)
&=\nabla_\beta \{y^{\mathrm T}X\beta + 1_n^{\mathrm T} \log(\sigma(-X\beta))\}\\
&= 
X^{\mathrm T}y + \sum_{i=1}^n \nabla_\beta \log(\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i))\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}y + \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)} \sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)[1-\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)]\cdot (-x_i)\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}y - \sum_{i=1}^n \sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)x_i\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}y - X^{\mathrm T}\sigma(X\beta)\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}(y-\sigma(X\beta))
\end{align}
$$
要证明在给定样本下 Logistic 回归没有极大似然解，  
等价于证明优化问题 $\max_{\beta\in \mathbb R^2}\mathcal L(y|X,\beta)$ 无解，  
只需证明 $\mathcal L(y|X,\beta)$ 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中没有驻点，  
即要证明 $\nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta) = X^{\mathrm T}(y-\sigma(X\beta))$ 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中没有零点，  
即要证明对于任意 $\beta\in \mathbb R^2$，$y-\sigma(X\beta)$ 都不与 $X^{\mathrm T}$ 的列空间正交.

*****

回忆起设计矩阵和响应变量观测为:  
$$
(x_i,y_i) = 
\begin{cases}
(0, 1) & \text{if }i=1,\dots,10\\
(1, 1) & \text{if }i=11,\dots,15\\
(1, 0) & \text{if }i=16,\dots,20 
\end{cases}\\

X = 
\begin{bmatrix}
1_{10} & 0_{10}\\
1_{10} & 1_{10}
\end{bmatrix}
\quad
y=
\begin{bmatrix}
1_{15}\\
0_{5}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
我们记:  
$$
\begin{align}
a &:= \sigma \left(\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
0
\end{bmatrix}^{\mathrm T}
\beta\right)\\
b &:= 
\sigma \left(\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
1
\end{bmatrix}^{\mathrm T}\beta\right)
\end{align}
$$
根据 Logistic 函数 $\sigma(\cdot)$ 的性质可知 $a,b\in (0,1)$  
于是我们有:  
$$
y_i -\sigma(x_i^{\mathrm T}\beta) = 
\begin{cases}
1-a & \text{if }i=1,\dots,10\\
1-b & \text{if }i=11,\dots,15\\
-b & \text{if }i=16,\dots,20
\end{cases}
$$
因此方程 $X^{\mathrm T}(y-\sigma(X\beta))=0_2$  可以表示为:  
$$
\begin{cases}
10(1-a) + 5(1-b) + 5\cdot(-b) =15-10a-10b= 0\\
5(1-b) + 5\cdot (-b) = 5 - 10b = 0
\end{cases}
$$
解得 $\begin{cases}
a = 1\\
b = \frac12\end{cases}$   
这与 $a,b\in (0,1)$ 的事实相矛盾，因此不存在 $\beta\in \mathbb R^2$ 使得 $X^{\mathrm T}(y-\sigma(X\beta))=0_2$  
结合前面的推理可知在本题所给的样本下，Logistic 回归不存在极大似然解.



## Problem 5

Consider the maximation of the ridge penalized loglikelihood of logistic regression:  
$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)
&=
y^{\mathrm T}X\beta + 1_n^{\mathrm T}\log(\sigma(-X\beta)) - \frac12 \lambda\|\beta\|^2\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n
\left\{
y_i(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i) + \log(\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i))
\right\} - \frac12 \lambda \|\beta\|^2\\
&=
\sum_{i=1}^n \left\{y_i(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i) - \log(1+\exp(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i))\right\} - \frac12 \lambda\|\beta\|^2
\end{align}
$$
where $X=[x_1,\dots,x_n]^{\mathrm T}\in \mathbb R^{n\times (p+1)}$, $\beta\in \mathbb R^{p+1}$ and $\lambda>0$   
Derive the Newton algorithm.  
(Hint: it is similar to the iteratively re-weighted least squares algorithm for unpenalized logistic regression)

**Solution:**  
首先求解 $\mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)$ 关于 $\beta$ 的梯度:  
$$
\begin{align}
\nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)
&=\nabla_\beta \{y^{\mathrm T}X\beta + 1_n^{\mathrm T} \log(\sigma(-X\beta))\} - \lambda \beta\\
&= 
X^{\mathrm T}y + \sum_{i=1}^n \nabla_\beta \log(\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)) - \lambda \beta\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}y + \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)} \sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)[1-\sigma(-\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)]\cdot (-x_i)
- \lambda \beta\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}y - \sum_{i=1}^n \sigma(\beta^{\mathrm T}x_i)x_i- \lambda \beta\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}y - X^{\mathrm T}\sigma(X\beta)- \lambda \beta\\
&=
X^{\mathrm T}(y-\sigma(X\beta))- \lambda \beta
\end{align}
$$
其次求解 $\mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)$ 关于 $\beta$ 的 Hesse 矩阵:
$$
\begin{align}
\nabla_\beta^2 \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)
&=
\frac{\partial }{\partial \beta}\{ \nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda) \}\\
&=
\frac{\partial }{\partial \beta}\{X^{\mathrm T}(y-\sigma(X\beta))- \lambda \beta\}\\
&=
- X^{\mathrm T} \cdot \text{diag}\{\sigma(X\beta) \odot (1_n - \sigma(X\beta))\}\cdot X - \lambda I_{p+1}
\end{align}
$$

***

这样我们就可以给出加入 $l_2$ 惩罚项的纯 Newton 法的迭代算法:  

- ① 初始化 $\beta^{(0)}= 0_{d+1}$ 

- ② 然后迭代更新参数直至达到某个预设定的停止条件:   
  $$
  \begin{align}
  p^{(k)}
  &= \sigma(X\beta^{(k)})\\
  \nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda) 
  &= X^{\mathrm T}(y-p^{(k)}) - \lambda \beta^{(k)}\\
  
  \nabla_\beta^2 \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)
  &= 
  -X^{\mathrm T}\cdot \text{diag}\{(1_n-p^{(k)})\odot p^{(k)}\}\cdot X - \lambda I_{p+1}\\
  d^{(k)} 
  &= (\nabla_\beta^2 \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda))^{-1} \nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)\\
  \beta^{(k+1)} &= \beta^{(k)} + d^{(k)}
  \end{align}
  $$
  (注意这是一个最大化问题，因此 $d^{(k)}$ 这里代表 "上升方向"，对应最小化问题中的下降方向)

****

写成加权最小二乘格式即为:  
$$
\begin{align}
\beta^{(k+1)} 
&= 
\beta^{(k)} + d^{(k)}\\
&= 
\beta^{(k)} + (\nabla_\beta^2 \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda))^{-1} \nabla_\beta \mathcal L(y|X,\beta,\lambda)\\
&=
\beta^{(k)} + [-X^{\mathrm T}\cdot \text{diag}\{(1_n-p^{(k)})\odot p^{(k)}\}\cdot X - \lambda I_{p+1}]^{-1} 
[X^{\mathrm T}(y-p^{(k)}) - \lambda \beta^{(k)}]\\
&=
\beta^{(k)} + (X^{\mathrm T}W_k X + \lambda I_{p+1})^{-1}X^{\mathrm T} W_k z^{(k)}\\
\end{align}
$$
其中 $W_k = \text{diag}\{(1_n-p^{(k)})\odot p^{(k)}\} = \text{diag}\{(1_n-\sigma(X\beta^{(k)}))\odot \sigma(X\beta^{(k)})\}$   
而 $z^{(k)} = W_k^{-1} [(y-p^{(k)}) - X(X^{\mathrm T}X)^{-1}\beta^{(k)}]$ 

**The End**
